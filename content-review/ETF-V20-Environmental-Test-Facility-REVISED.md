## Why It Matters

### To Individual People (Tour Visitors)
Lunar hardware faces a brutal combination: hard vacuum, extreme temperature swings from sunlight to shadow, abrasive dust that gets into every mechanism, and lighting so harsh it can hide the ground itself. V20 recreates all of it in one chamber - not just thermal vacuum, but actual regolith simulant and lighting that mimics the South Pole sun - so rovers, landers, and equipment can prove they'll survive the Moon before they ever leave Earth.

### To NASA/Industry/The World

**To NASA Programs:** V20 is a SETMO Tier 1 Asset fulfilling a NASA mission priority need for lunar surface environment testing. Artemis program hardware, commercial lunar landers, and Moon-to-Mars systems all depend on this capability. Catching dust infiltration, thermal cycling failures, or mechanism problems here saves missions - and potentially crew lives.

**To Industry Partners:** Commercial access to V20 (like Starpath's rover test) enables startups and companies to qualify lunar hardware without building their own multi-million dollar lunar simulation facility. Levels the playing field for the commercial lunar economy.

**To The World:** Every piece of hardware bound for the lunar surface must prove it can survive regolith, vacuum, temperature extremes, and harsh lighting before it flies. V20 is where that proof happens - NASA's world-class testbed for lunar and planetary surface environments.

# SECTION 2: SCIENCE (How It Works)

## Official Capability Description (from Power Apps)

**[VERIFIED]** Environmental Test Facility (ETF) - Extracted 2026-08-24:

The Environmental Test Facility (ETF) is a highly utilized test facility at MSFC made up of multiple test chambers that share a common, highly skilled workforce. The ETF provides NASA with ground test capabilities for development, qualification, and acceptance testing of space flight hardware utilizing multiple thermal vacuum, humidity, and altitude chambers capable of extreme vacuum and temperature environments & simulated altitudes to 100,000 ft. Includes the V20 chamber which includes a lunar regolith test option.

**V20 Chamber Specific (from tour app prototype):**

The largest TVAC chamber in the Environmental Test Facility, V20, has expanded its TVAC capability to include full scale hardware testing in a lunar surface environment and fulfills a NASA mission priority need for both the agency and industry. The lunar regolith testing capability of V20 provides NASA and its partners a world class testbed for hardware exposure in a lunar (and eventually planetary) surface environment. V20 is a NASA Space Environments Testing Management Office (SETMO) Tier 1 Asset and features two high capacity diffusion pumps, a negative air pressure regolith containment tent for safe handling / operations, respirator trained technicians for test article handling in a "Dirty" environment, and a cart & rail system with 60K lb. load capacity.

## Current "Deep Dive" Content

**Title:** Go deeper: how V20 simulates the lunar surface

**Content:**
Starpath, a small technology startup, won second place overall at NASA's Break the Ice Lunar Challenge and brought its rover to V20 for a real test in lunar-like conditions. Engineers built a concrete slab with sandy, rocky terrain to mimic the Moon's surface, slid it into the chamber alongside the rover on V20's 60,000-lb capacity cart, and cycled the chamber's lamps on and off to recreate the harsh lighting changes of the lunar South Pole environment.

V20 was expanded in 2022 specifically for this mission. Two 32-inch diffusion pumps were added to handle the gas loads from regolith operations - sintering, additive manufacturing, and dust mitigation testing. A negative-pressure dust containment tent encloses the chamber's working area, and respirator-trained technicians handle test articles in this deliberately "dirty" environment. The chamber can reach vacuum levels of 5×10⁻⁷ Torr while cycling temperatures from -170°C to +200°C, with a liquid nitrogen shroud and IR lamps recreating the thermal extremes of sunlight and shadow.

The regolith simulant itself is engineered to match lunar soil properties - particle size, abrasiveness, electrostatic behavior. Hardware doesn't just experience vacuum and temperature here; it experiences the Moon: dust infiltrating seals, thermal cycling stressing joints, and lighting conditions that can hide obstacles and slopes.

## Technical Capabilities Summary

**Context:** V20 is the largest of 18 chambers in the Environmental Test Facility (ETF). For complete chamber listing and ETF facility overview, see Related Stops section below.

### V20 Thermal Vacuum Chamber [VERIFIED from multiple sources]

**Overall Dimensions (from V-20 One Pager 100323):**
- **Chamber Size:** 20 ft diameter × 28 ft deep
- **Internal Usable Space:** See CAD diagrams (167" vertical clearance, 108" usable width on cart)
- **Cart & Rail System:** 60,000 lb load capacity, winch-operated from outside chamber

**Temperature & Vacuum (from V-20 One Pager + ETF One Pager):**
- **Temperature Range:** -170°C to +200°C
- **Vacuum Pressure:** 5×10⁻⁷ Torr
- **Thermal Shroud:** LN₂ (liquid nitrogen) filled or heated GN₂ (gaseous nitrogen)
- **IR Lamps:** Heated shroud + IR lamps for temperature control

**Vacuum System (from V-20 One Pager):**
- **Diffusion Pumps:** Two 32-inch high-capacity diffusion pumps (added for lunar regolith operations)
- **Cryogenic Pump:** One 36-inch cryopump (for flight tests or non-regolith operations)
- **Turbomolecular Pumps:** Three maglev turbomolecular pumps
- **Cold Plates:** Two large 20 ft² cold plates for contamination control (handle large gas loads)

**Instrumentation (from V-20 One Pager):**
- **Thermocouples:** 486 baselined
- **Data System:** National Instruments PXIE-1084 Chassis (1Hz baselined, 10Hz max)
- **RGA:** Yes (Residual Gas Analyzer)
- **TQCM:** Yes (Temperature Controlled Quartz Crystal Microbalance)
- **Cameras:** IR and color camera/video
- **CAD File:** Available upon request

**Power & Feedthroughs (from V-20 One Pager):**
- **Primary Power:** 40-amp 0-240Vac with automatic control
- **Available Voltages:** 12Vdc, 24Vdc, 208Vac, 240Vac, 480Vac
- **Blank Flanges:** Several penetrate shroud for test article instrumentation & cabling feedthroughs

**Lunar Regolith Capability (from V-20 One Pager + Power Apps):**
- **Capability Added:** 2022 (expanded to simulate lunar & planetary environments)
- **Dust Containment:** Negative air pressure dust containment tent encloses chamber working area
- **Personnel:** Respirator trained technicians for "Dirty" environment operations
- **Regolith Operations:** Sintering, additive manufacturing, dust mitigation for mechanisms and structures
- **Regolith Bed:** Highly customizable regolith simulant beds on removable cart
- **Mission:** Support NASA Moon to Mars Program

**SETMO Status (from Power Apps):**
- **Designation:** NASA Space Environments Testing Management Office (SETMO) Tier 1 Asset
- **Significance:** World class testbed for hardware exposure in lunar (and eventually planetary) surface environment
