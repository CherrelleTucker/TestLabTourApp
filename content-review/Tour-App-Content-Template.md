## INSTRUCTIONS FOR COMPLETING THIS TEMPLATE

**Purpose:** This document verifies tour stop content against official sources and identifies gaps for SME review. The SME receives a clean, professional document showing verified facts and specific questions - not our working notes.

### Content Development Rules

**1. SOURCE HIERARCHY (What to trust)**
   - ✅ **Official one-pagers** (with NASA pub numbers) - HIGHEST AUTHORITY
   - ✅ **Power Apps database** - Official capability descriptions, business units, pursuits
   - ✅ **NASA Image and Video Library** (images.nasa.gov) - Cleared for public
   - ✅ **NTRS / nasa.gov / MSFC channels** - Verified technical documents
   - ❌ **Tour app prototype** - NEVER use as source of truth. This is DRAFT CONTENT being verified, not a reference.

**2. SCOPE FOCUS**
   - This document covers ONE TOUR STOP (typically 15 minutes, 15-20 people)
   - Focus on what visitors see at THIS stop, not the entire facility/branch
   - Example: V20 tour stop = V20 chamber content. ETF facility overview belongs in "Related Stops" section.
   - Keep narration, specs, and deep dive laser-focused on the specific capability being toured

**3. STYLE & FORMATTING**
   - ❌ **Never use em dashes (—)** - Use regular hyphens (-) instead
   - ❌ **Never include [CORRECTED], [REVISED], [UPDATED] markers** - SME doesn't need to see our process notes
   - ✅ **Use [VERIFIED]** when extracted from official sources
   - ✅ **Use [SME INPUT NEEDED]** for gaps that require SME expertise
   - ✅ **Use [NEEDS VERIFICATION]** for information that exists but needs SME confirmation

**4. DISCREPANCY HANDLING**
   - When official sources conflict with each other, present both and ask SME to clarify
   - Don't speculate about why numbers differ or where draft content came from
   - Don't explain our verification process to the SME - just present facts and gaps

**5. VERIFICATION WORKFLOW**
   1. Extract Power Apps data (capability description, business units, pursuits)
   2. Read all available one-pagers in OnePagers folder
   3. Document NASA pub numbers from one-pagers (if visible)
   4. Cross-check technical specs across sources
   5. Add suggested enhancements directly into content (don't create "suggestions" sections)
   6. Mark verified info clearly, identify gaps for SME vs. App Dev Team
   7. Keep "Related Stops" section updated with facility context
   8. Attribute media/clearance tasks to `[APP DEV TEAM]`, not SME

**6. RESPONSIBILITY SEPARATION**
   
   **[SME INPUT NEEDED] - Questions requiring branch/facility expertise:**
   - Timeline dates (facility construction, major upgrades, test campaigns)
   - Hardware tested (specific programs, years, mission details)
   - Technical verification (chamber counts, specs still current?)
   - Facility relationships (ETF vs XRCF, organizational structure)
   - Sensitive information flags (ITAR, export control, unreleased programs)
   - Confirmation of suggested tags/questions appropriateness
   
   **[APP DEV TEAM] - Our responsibility, not SME's:**
   - Photo/media attribution and photographer credits
   - Clearance status verification (images.nasa.gov, NASA Image Library)
   - NASA pub number research
   - Media requests and sourcing
   - Content integration after SME feedback
   - Tour app technical implementation
   - Cross-stop navigation and "Related Stops" links
   
   **Don't create "Suggested enhancements" sections** - just add the enhancement directly to content unless there's a specific reason not to. SME reviews final content, not suggestions about content.

**7. MARKING CONVENTIONS**
   - `[VERIFIED]` - Extracted from official sources, documented
   - `[SME INPUT NEEDED]` - Requires branch/facility expertise
   - `[APP DEV TEAM]` - Our responsibility, tracked here for completeness
   - `[NEEDS VERIFICATION]` - Information exists but needs SME confirmation
   - ❌ Never use: `[CORRECTED]`, `[REVISED]`, `[UPDATED]` - SME doesn't need our process notes
